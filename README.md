Understanding the Essentials of AI

Before diving into the specifics of the course, it’s crucial to grasp what artificial intelligence entails. AI refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. This encompasses learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. For developers, understanding these fundamentals is key to harnessing the power of AI.
